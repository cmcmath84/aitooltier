Backlink Directory Battle Plan

55 real places to list aitooltier.com, ranked by bot-detection difficulty and by whether the link is actually worth chasing for a domain that's recovering from deindexing. Filter, work top-down, skip the farms.

⚠ Read this before submitting to anything

AIToolTier is recovering from a deindexing event. On a domain in that state, blasting 55 directories is the wrong move — a flood of low-quality links looks like manipulation and can set the recovery back. The winning play is the opposite: pursue the S and A tiers only (real editorial/AI directories with genuine traffic), skip the C-tier grind until indexing recovers, and never touch the link-farm networks in the last section. Quality and slow velocity beat volume here.

04 Do NOT submit here — link farms that hurt recovery

✕
neobacklinks.net network (zendirectory, poec, coastradar, besttopdir…) — auto-generated PBN-style directories. Textbook toxic links.
✕
"350+/500+ Free DA-PA directory submission" lists (dsom.in, futuregenapps, saalinko, serpmaestro) — mass generic web directories, zero relevance, spammy footprint.
✕
Done-for-you "submit to 100+ directories" services (launchdirectories, effortlessbacklinks paid tiers) — the exact velocity/pattern that flags manipulation on a recovering domain.
✕
Generic business-listing farms (freefind, guestbook-free, promotebusinessdirectory) — built for local SEO spam, not an AI product.
